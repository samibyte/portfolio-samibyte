**Goal:** replace the flat gradient water with a split-level tropical sea that reads as a camera at the waterline: 30% luminous sky, 70% water, strong near-field flow, clear light play, depth, and particles/creatures passing in front of the lens. Photographic and volumetric, not a CSS gradient with sine stripes.

## Locked constraints

- **C1:** At rest, `uWaterline` mean = `0.7` GL (30% from top); scroll adds `scrollY / vh` 1:1. The mask is computed from **undisplaced** `vUv` — `wl` from `vUv.x`, `under` from `vUv.y`. No refraction, ripple, roll, turb or displacement may move it; those apply to colour sampling only. Sum of up to 4 sine terms, envelope at `x=1%` clamped to **±0.024**, so the boundary stays inside 27–33% (0.676–0.724). The foam/spec edge must be the largest luminance step in that column: runner-up step ≤ 2/3 of it.
- **C2:** At 100%, mean luminance < 0.05 and mean B > mean R. Every additive layer fades with depth; abyss keeps only the existing glow and very light grain. No near-lens particles, fauna or strong rays at 100%.
- **C3:** Springs untouched.
- **C4:** `npx tsx scripts/contrast.ts` exits 0 and `tests/c4.spec.ts` passes. Light-text zones may receive at most +0.06 shader lift. New additive layers (rays, caustics, particles, fauna, grade) are capped by the local background luma: where it is < 0.2, total additive lift ≤ 0.06. Never lower a threshold.
- **C5:** One rAF loop in `lib/signal.ts`. All shader motion is `uTime`/uniform-driven. Native scroll, no `preventDefault` on wheel/touch, no `transform` on `<main>`.
- **C6:** No new fixed chrome; reachability unchanged.
- **C7:** 60 fps is **U**. Prove tier mapping, no second loop, and `tests/c7.spec.ts` green. **Tier 1** (adaptive-low, DPR 1) drops fauna and camera/grade and halves near particles. Tier 2 inherits those cuts and is cheaper still. Tier 3 is static: all layers present, `uTime = 0`. Tier 4 has no canvas.
- **C8:** Content untouched.
- **SPEC §6:** GLSL ES 1.00, no `#version`, `Triangle` `vec2` attributes, **≤12 noise taps/pixel at `uOct = 4`**, constant loop bounds with uniform cutoffs, no dynamic uniform-array indexing, no per-frame allocation. Keep the `under`/sky composition and `uDebug`. Layer codes are `11..18`; `1` remains the under-mask.
- **SPEC §7:** Cut order stays DPR → `uOct` → rays → caustics → snow → glow → flow. New layers cut only in this order: near particles → camera/grade → fauna → far particles. Never cut the waterline, foam, specular or palette.
- **Numbers:** amplitudes, counts, speeds, fade depths, alphas and thresholds live in `lib/tuning.ts` and are wired as uniforms (pack pairs in `vec4` where natural). Required keys: `waveAmp`, `waveFreq0..3`, `bandDepth`, `skyHorizon`, `warpNear`, `warpFar`, `causticCells`, `rayCount`, `particleFar`, `particleMid`, `particleNear`, `dropletCount`, `faunaSchools`, `faunaJellies`, `faunaAlpha`, `vignette`, `grain`, `drift`, `roll`. - **No new dependency.** Keep `ogl@1.0.11`; no three.js.
- **No `setState` per frame; no `getComputedStyle` during render.** Tier and debug flags change only on discrete events.

## Look

**Sky (30%).** Pale-blue vertical gradient, soft sun glow top-right, one 3-octave fbm reused for two drifting cloud bands (light-only, ≤ 0.04 contrast behind the hero text), subtle horizon haze. Refraction near the waterline distorts only the colour sample in a ±2% band, never the mask.

**Surface/waterline.** 3–4 summed wave frequencies (sin, not noise), envelope clamped to ±0.024. Bright specular glints and thin foam on crests, using the flow noise sample. A rippled bright band just below the line fades over 15% of viewport height. The foam edge stays crisp enough to win the C1 step test.

**Refraction field.** 2–3 octave domain-warped fbm displacing the colour sample of everything underwater, never the mask or zone gradient. Advected with `uTime` and boosted by scroll velocity and `uTurb`. Near-field amplitude is visibly larger than far-field (bottom vs top of the water). No repeating sine ribbons.

**Caustics.** Analytic warped lattice, 10 cells across (uniform), strongest in the top half, multiply-only on water, fading to zero by twilight/midnight/abyss. Reuses the warp noise; zero extra taps.

**God rays.** 7 wide, noise-broken additive beams (uniform count) from the sun, strongest near the surface, fading with depth, gated by `uFx.x`, no visible banding.

**Particles (three parallax layers + droplets).** Far 14 tiny at 0.5× scroll, mid 7 at 1×, near 4 large soft blurred discs at 1.8× plus 3 lens-wet droplets/streaks that pass in front of the camera, moving opposite to scroll. Constant loop bounds 24/12/6/4 with uniform cutoffs. All fade with depth via the zone palette, so abyss shows none. Near alpha ≤ 0.25 and never the largest edge in the `x=1%` column. This replaces marine-snow semantics while keeping `uFx.z` and tier gating.

**Fauna.** 5 small fish schools and 2 jellyfish as soft SDF silhouettes with time-driven tail wiggle, fogged to local water colour, alpha ≤ 0.3, slow paths, fewer with depth, zero in abyss. Soft edges, slight motion trail, no sticker look. Total analytic shapes ≤ 30, one constant-bound loop.

**Camera and grade.** Slow x-only drift, roll ≤ 0.2°, mild vignette (≤ 6% corner falloff), very light grain (≤ 0.01), subtle tonal variation. No vertical drift, no rotation of the mask, no geometric/barrel distortion. Reduced motion freezes all of it.

**“In front of the lens” means here:** foreground parallax, larger near-field refraction toward the bottom, soft-focus near bokeh, lens-wet droplets, caustic light on the foreground, depth fog. Not a distant flat ocean.

**Reject:** neon cyan, hard rainbow bands, evenly spaced sine ribbons, flat vector dots, perfect geometric circles, uniform “stars”, posterized gradients, anything that looks like a CSS gradient with noise.

### Noise budget (comment table in `shader.ts`)

| Layer | Taps | Reuse |
|---|---|---|
| Flow fbm | 4 (`uOct`) | base field; reused by specular and caustic warp |
| Refraction warp | 2 | one `noise` per axis at a different scale |
| Clouds | 3 | one 3-octave fbm reused for both bands |
| God rays | 1 | breaks the beam edge |
| Particle flutter | 1 | shared by far/mid/near/droplets |
| Caustics | 0 | warp + analytic lattice |
| Glow | 0 | sine wander replaces the old noise call |
| **Total** | **11** | ≤ 12 at `uOct = 4`, 8 at `uOct = 2` |
