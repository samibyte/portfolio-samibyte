# P4 — Physics and interaction

**Goal:** scroll velocity and pointer movement feel like water, and the spring is proven frame-rate independent.
**Read:** `SPEC.md` §3.2, §7.
**Exit:** C3 test passes at 1/60 s and 1/30 s steps; no layout shift; no jank when changing scroll speed.

## P4.1 Spring unit test

**Files:** `lib/signal.ts` (only if `stepSpring` needs a fix), `tests/c3.spec.ts`.
Feed `stepSpring` a step input for 3 s at 1/60 s and at 1/30 s. Assert: it overshoots (max value > target), then decays so that `|x - target| < 5%` of peak within 3 s. Compare the two step rates on the **peak** and the **settled value** (each within 10%); do not compare the pointwise transient — Euler at 1/60 s and 1/30 s legitimately diverges there (~23% at the overshoot).
The function must be pure — `stepSpring(x, v, target, dt)` returns `{ x, v }`. No `T` mutation inside.
**Done:** `npx playwright test tests/c3.spec.ts` passes.

## P4.2 Turbulence

**Files:** `components/ocean/OceanCanvas.tsx`.
Scroll velocity in px/s → `/ T.velocityNorm` → clamp to ±1 → the scroll spring. `uTurb = |x|`. Clamp `dt` (`T.dtMax`) so a background tab cannot produce a spike.
**Done:** a fast scroll produces visible distortion that settles within 3 s; reversing direction does not produce a discontinuity.

## P4.3 Pointer

**Files:** `components/ocean/OceanCanvas.tsx`, `lib/signal.ts`.
One `pointermove` listener, passive, on `window`. Store raw `x/y` in a ref-free module field; the loop converts to `uMouse` (`y` flipped) and moves a second spring for `uMouseVel`. No `setState`, no `getBoundingClientRect` per frame (cache the canvas rect on resize).
**Done:** ripple follows the pointer with a slight lag and settles; two pointers (touch) do not throw.
